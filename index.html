About the Firm

HSK Engineering and Architectural Solutions bridges the gap between robust structural engineering and innovative architectural design. Our Services

Structural Analysis & Engineering

Expert evaluation and mathematical modeling of structural components. Specializing in resolving boundary conditions and determining reaction components for structural layouts.

3D Modeling & BIM

Comprehensive building information modeling using industry-standard tools including Revit and SketchUp to visualize complex structural and architectural concepts.

Computer-Aided Design (CAD)

Precision 2D drafting and planar view layouts utilizing AutoCAD. We deliver clean, accurate, and professional documentation for all phases of construction and design.
